## Welcome to BUCC Dev

We build the software that runs campus life at Babcock University.

BUCC Dev is the engineering arm of the Babcock University Computer Club. Students ship real products here, used by real students, and learn by building in the open.

## Products

- [The Babcock Shop](https://github.com/bucc-dev/THE_BABCOCK_SHOP) · campus commerce for food, essentials, and services
- [Babcock Shop Core](https://github.com/bucc-dev/THE_BABCOCK_SHOP_CORE) · the commerce engine behind The Babcock Shop
- [BUCC Applications](https://github.com/bucc-dev/apply.wearebucc) · join a BUCC committee
- Findr · campus lost and found, coming soon
